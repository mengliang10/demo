# Comprehensive Compendium of Data Visualization & Presentation Frameworks

A definitive, multi-language reference guide cataloging visualization, graphical, and presentation frameworks across **JavaScript, Python, R, Julia, Rust, C++, C, Go, SQL, PHP, Scala, and Visual Basic**, as well as specialized scientific, mathematical, physics, chemistry, AI/ML, fintech, and creative coding domains.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **100% Free & Open-Source Guarantee (FOSS)**: Every single framework cataloged in this document is strictly **free of charge** and distributed under recognized open-source and free licenses (such as **MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD-2/3-Clause, GNU GPL/LGPL, zlib, EPL, ISC, and Public Domain**). Zero commercial-only, proprietary, closed-source, or paywalled software is included.

All frameworks are categorized by language or domain and **sorted from most popular to least popular** (based on industry adoption, GitHub stars, and package ecosystem gravity).
