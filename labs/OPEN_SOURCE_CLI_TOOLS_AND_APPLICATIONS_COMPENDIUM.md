# Comprehensive Compendium of Production-Grade Open-Source CLI Tools & Applications

A definitive reference guide cataloging battle-tested, command-line executable open-source applications and analytical frameworks across **Marketing, Finance, Machine Learning & AI, Data Engineering, Scientific Computing, Mathematics, Bioinformatics, Geospatial / GIS, Graphics & Creative Media, Observability & BI, and DevOps / Cybersecurity**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Production & Governance Criteria**:
> Every tool cataloged in this document satisfies four strict criteria:
> 1. **100% Free & Open-Source Software (FOSS)**: Permissive or copyleft open-source licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD, GPL/LGPL, MPL, etc.).
> 2. **Native CLI Executability**: Provides a first-class command-line binary, CLI runner, or scriptable terminal interface.
> 3. **Institutional Governance**: Actively maintained by a reputable company, open-source foundation (Apache, Linux Foundation, CNCF, OSGeo, NumFOCUS), government research agency (NASA, NIH, CERN, ESA), or active community (>5 maintainers).
> 4. **Active Maintenance**: Regularly updated, active repository commits, and modern release cycles.

All tools are organized by domain and **sorted from most popular to least popular** (based on enterprise adoption, GitHub stars, and package ecosystem gravity).

> [!NOTE]
> **Industry Context on Uber & Airbnb in Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)**:
> • **Uber**: Open-sourced two flagship marketing and causal inference engines:
>   1. **Orbit (`orbit-ml`)**: Specifically created and benchmarked by Uber's data science team for Bayesian time series modeling, marketing campaign response, and Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM).
>   2. **CausalML (`causalml`)**: Uber's industrial library for uplift modeling and causal machine learning, estimating the incremental ROI of marketing treatments, discounts, and ad campaigns.
> • **Airbnb**: Airbnb has **not** open-sourced a standalone named MMM framework repository (unlike Meta's Robyn or Google's Meridian). However, Airbnb is famous for its published Bayesian MMM methodology (using hierarchical Bayesian models in Stan and PyMC to measure brand vs. performance marketing), and open-sourced the underlying data and orchestration platforms that power such workflows: **Apache Airflow** (orchestration), **Apache Superset** (BI visualization), **Chronon** (ML feature platform), and **Knowledge Repo** (reproducible research sharing).
