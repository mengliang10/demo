# Tours, Activities & Experiences Distribution Ecosystem & Economics

An interactive, multi-tier data visualization dashboard mapping the complete global tours, activities, attractions, and experiences journey, ResTech connectivity platforms, experience OTAs, cruise shore excursions, and operator financial revenue/cost splits.

---

## Quick Start: Launching the Tours Dashboard

Open [`index.html`](file:///run/media/ml/Storage/Labs/tours/index.html) in your browser, or launch a local web server:

```bash
cd "/run/media/ml/Storage/Labs/tours"
python3 -m http.server 8083
```

Then navigate to: **`http://localhost:8083`**

## Key Experiences & Attractions Economic Metrics

The model uses a global tours, activities, and attractions baseline of **$160.0 Billion USD** in Gross Booking Value (GBV), representing **1.4 Billion annual bookings** globally at a blended ticket spend of **$114.28** per booking.

| Metric | Amount ($B) | Share (%) | Experiences Industry Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Global Experience GBV** | **$160.0B** | **100.0%** | Total spend across attractions, day tours, outdoor adventure & multi-day trips |
| **Direct Channels (Offline + Web)** | **$90.0B** | **56.3%** | On-site box offices & turnstiles ($50B) + ResTech direct web widgets ($40B) |
| **Intermediated (OTAs, Shorex, DMCs)** | **$70.0B** | **43.7%** | Experience OTAs ($38B), Cruise Shorex ($12B), Inbound DMCs ($12B), Concierge ($8B) |
| **Distribution & Intermediary Friction** | **$27.4B** | **17.1%** | Highest commission friction in travel! (Viator/GYG 25-30%, Shorex 45%+) |
| **Net Operator Revenue** | **$132.6B** | **82.9%** | Net retained by activity operators and attraction facilities |
| **Operator Operating Expenses** | **$104.2B** | **65.1%** | Guides/staff labor (29%), vehicles/boats/fuel (18%), park permits (12%), insurance (7%) |
| **Operator Operating Profit (EBITDA)** | **$28.4B** | **17.8%** | 21.4% margin on Net Operator Revenue |

## 5-Tier Experiences Journey Architecture

1. **Tier 0: Operator & Attraction Supply ($160.0B)**: Iconic Attractions & Theme Parks ($55.0B / 34.4%), Independent Day Tour Operators ($60.0B / 37.5%), Outdoor & Adventure Sports ($25.0B / 15.6%), Multi-Day Expeditions ($20.0B / 12.5%).
2. **Tier 1: Tech & ResTech Infrastructure ($160.0B)**: Cloud ResTech Platforms (FareHarbor, Bókun, Peek Pro, Rezdy) ($75.0B), Turnstiles & Gates (Roller, Gateway) ($45.0B), Offline POS & Cash ($25.0B), Tour Operator ERPs (Tourplan) ($15.0B).
3. **Tier 2: Primary Channels ($160.0B)**: On-Site Box Office & Walk-up ($50.0B / 31.3%), Direct Website & Mobile ($40.0B / 25.0%), Experience Mega-OTAs ($38.0B / 23.8%), Cruise Line Shorex ($12.0B / 7.5%), Inbound DMCs ($12.0B / 7.5%), Hotel Concierge ($8.0B / 5.0%).
4. **Tier 3: Intermediaries & Front-Ends ($160.0B)**: Physical Box Offices & Turnstiles, ResTech Web Checkouts, OTA Consumer Marketplaces, Shorex Desks, DMC Desks, Concierge Desks.
5. **Tier 4: Guest & Visitor Segments ($160.0B)**: Independent Sightseers (FIT) ($65.0B / 40.6%), Family Vacationers & Theme Parks ($40.0B / 25.0%), Adventure Seekers ($18.0B / 11.3%), Cruise Ship Day Visitors ($15.0B / 9.4%), Multi-Day Cultural Tourists ($14.0B / 8.8%), Corporate Teambuilding ($8.0B / 5.0%).
